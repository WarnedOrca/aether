## The idea

Most small businesses have one task someone does by hand every day or week: reminder texts, follow-ups after a visit, telling customers an item is back in stock, chasing a late payment. I'm building a free tool that does the boring part of one task like that, and you stay in charge of everything.

## What you would get

- A 10-minute conversation about one task that eats your time.
- A one-page, plain-English description of exactly what the tool would do for that task: which messages it would write, to whom, when, and what it would never do.
- A free web page that runs on your own computer, with no install and no internet. You load your own customer list (exported from your spreadsheet or booking app) and it prepares the messages for the people who are due, using your rules. You read each one, approve or skip it, and send the approved ones from your own phone or email with one tap.
- Your own rules written in: who may be contacted, what must never appear in a message, and any money limits.

## What stays the same

- **You approve every message** before it could go to a customer. You can change or reject any of them.
- **Your customer list never leaves your computer.** The page doesn't upload anything, and I never need to see your list. You can try it first on a file of made-up customers.
- **It's free**, and you can stop at any time.

## What it can't do yet

It's an early student project. It doesn't connect to your booking app, spreadsheet or phone: you export a list, and **it never sends anything by itself**, so you press send. It only reads US phone numbers. If a row is unclear (no phone number, an unreadable date), it skips that customer and tells you why rather than guessing. I can't promise it will save you time; if you like, we can time the task before and after to find out.
