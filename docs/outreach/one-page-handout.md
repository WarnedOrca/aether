## The idea

Most small businesses have one task someone does by hand every day or week: reminder texts, follow-ups after a visit, telling customers an item is back in stock, chasing a late payment. I'm building a free tool that does the boring part of one task like that, and you stay in charge of everything.

## What you would get

- A 10-minute conversation about one task that eats your time.
- A one-page, plain-English description of exactly what the tool would do for that task: which messages it would write, to whom, when, and what it would never do.
- A test run on made-up sample data, so you can see the messages it would draft before anything is real.
- Your own rules written in: who may be contacted, what must never appear in a message, and any money limits.

## What stays the same

- **You approve every message** before it could go to a customer. You can change or reject any of them.
- **No customer information is needed** to start. The test run uses made-up data.
- **It's free**, and you can stop at any time.

## What it can't do yet

It's an early student project. Right now it doesn't connect to your booking app, spreadsheet or phone, so it can't send anything for you yet. The first step is just understanding your task and showing you a test run. It can make mistakes, which is why you check everything first.
