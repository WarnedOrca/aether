# Free pilot: plain-language agreement (TEMPLATE, NOT LEGAL ADVICE)

This is a template. A parent or guardian must read and approve it before it is offered to anyone, and the business owner may want someone they trust to read it too. Fill in everything in [brackets]; delete what doesn't apply.

**Between** [business name], represented by [owner's name] ("the Business"), and [student's name], a student, with [parent or guardian's name] as parent or guardian ("the Student").

1. **What the pilot covers.** One task: [describe it in one sentence, e.g. "reminder texts for next-day appointments"]. Nothing else.

2. **Free.** No money changes hands, now or later. The Student uses only free tools.

3. **How it works.** The Business uses the draft pack page (`tool/draft-pack.html`) on its own computer. It loads its own customer list, which the page reads only inside the browser and never uploads. The page prepares draft messages using the Business's rules, and the Business approves, skips or sends each one itself from its own phone or email. The page never sends anything. The Student helps set it up and can show it first with made-up sample data. The Student does not receive the customer list unless the Business agrees to that in writing first.

4. **Data.**
   - The Business shares only what is listed here: [column names, or "none in phase 1"].
   - No customer personal data (names, phone numbers, emails, notes) is shared with the Student without the Business's written permission.
   - The Student does not copy business data to personal accounts or share it with anyone.
   - The Business can ask the Student to delete everything at any time, and the Student will confirm in writing when it is done.
   - Fields the Business marks as private (for example health or payment notes) are never put in a message.

5. **Human approval.** Nothing is sent to a customer, and no refund or order is made, unless the Business approves it first. The Business can approve, change or reject any message.

6. **Stopping.** Either side can stop the pilot at any time, for any reason, by telling the other. No explanation is needed.

7. **No guarantees.** This is a student project. The tool can make mistakes, and there is no promise that it will save time or work for this task. The Business reviews everything before it is used and is responsible for what it approves.

8. **Measuring.** With the Business's permission, the Student may write down how long the task took before and during the pilot (timed with a stopwatch where possible), how often it happens, and how many messages were late or missed; receive the page's audit log downloads, which hold counts, row numbers, reason codes and settings but no customer details; and write down the Business's own answers about no-shows, rebookings and, if the Business chooses to track it, revenue it believes came from the messages. A revenue figure is only ever described as "the owner reports", and only if the Business confirms it in writing. The Business decides whether its name may be mentioned in any write-up (school applications, a project report). Default: **not mentioned**.
