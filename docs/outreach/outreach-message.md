# Outreach messages (three versions)

Use these only with businesses on your own list, after a parent or guardian has seen the list and the messages. You send them yourself; adjust the words so they sound like you. Replace everything in [brackets]. Don't add claims the project can't back up (no "saves you hours", no "safe", no "AI that runs your business").

## Email

**Subject:** A free student project for [business name]

Hi [owner's name],

I'm [your name], a student at [school], and I'm looking for a small business willing to talk with me for about 10 minutes about one task they repeat by hand, like reminders or follow-ups.

I'm building a free tool that turns a task like that into a short program the owner can read in plain English. The owner approves every message before anything would go to a customer. It's an early student project: for now it only works on made-up sample data, so I wouldn't need any of your customer information.

If you're open to a short chat, I could come by [day or time window] or call whenever suits you. If not, no problem at all, and thanks for reading.

[your name]
[how to reach you: the contact route your parent or guardian has approved]

## Text

Hi [owner's name], it's [your name] ([how they know you]). I'm doing a free student project on taking repetitive tasks off small business owners' plates, with the owner approving everything. Could I ask you about one task you do by hand, about 10 min? No customer info needed, and totally fine if now's not a good time.

## In person

> Hi, I'm [your name]. I'm a student at [school], working on a free project for small businesses. I'm trying to learn about one task owners repeat by hand, like sending reminders or follow-ups, and whether a tool that drafts them for the owner to approve would help. Would you have ten minutes sometime this week or next? I don't need any customer information, and it's fine to say no.

If they're busy right then: "No problem. Could I leave this sheet and check back another day?" Then leave `one-page-handout.md` (printed) and go.
